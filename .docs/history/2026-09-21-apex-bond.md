# 2026-09-21: Apex Bond

Status: Implemented in source and data, build and runtime not verified

## Summary

Added Beast Mastery Hunter active spell 901046, Apex Bond. The spell requires a living active pet, heals the Hunter and pet for 15 percent maximum health, grants the pet 15 percent increased damage for 10 seconds, and has a 90-second cooldown.

## Produced

- `src/mod_apocalipse_hunter_apex_bond.cpp` with Bestial Wrath-style active and alive pet validation.
- `data/sql/db-world/2026_09_21_01_apex_bond.sql` with the guarded spell row, binding, non-save metadata, and backend name.
- Loader registration through `AddModApocalipseHunterApexBondScripts()`.
- Current behavior and verification scenarios in `.docs/custom-spells/apex-bond.md`.
- Architecture, operations, playerbot, catalog, and root documentation updates.

## Key decisions

- Implicit target 5, `TARGET_UNIT_PET`, supplies the same generic pet-presence contract used by Mend Pet.
- C++ additionally requires `Player::GetPet()` and a living pet so charms and dead pets cannot satisfy Apex Bond.
- Both heals use native percent-heal effects on their actual targets.
- The damage increase is a normal all-school damage aura on the pet rather than an owner-side pet-damage modifier.
- Acquisition and playerbot cast policy remain external.
